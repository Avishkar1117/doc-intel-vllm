# Phase 8 — SROIE: self-hosted vs Azure Document Intelligence (corrected, D-038)

Supersedes `phase8_writeup.md` (kept on record). **Nothing was re-run**: both capture files
(`phase8_sroie_capture.jsonl`, `phase8_sroie_di_capture.jsonl`) are unchanged, no GPU and no
DI calls. Only the scoring changed, in two places, both found by reading the saved outputs.

## What changed, and why it matters

1. **Dates no longer lose points for an appended time.** The model often writes
   `15/01/2019 11:05:16 AM` where gold has `15/01/2019`. The old matcher could not parse that
   and scored a correct calendar date as wrong. With a trailing clock time stripped from both
   gold and predicted, 59 of the model's 71 date misses were correct dates. The other 12 are
   real errors (for example `26-03-19` against `26-03-18`, or a truncated year). Self-hosted
   date F1 goes 0.794 to 0.965.
2. **DI's 21 "no Total" rows were three different things.** 15 are a genuinely missing
   `Total` field. 4 are DI answering with a wrong negative total (-45.0, -35.0, -15.0, -55.0
   against printed 408.45, 59.35, 47.15, 83.55); they were filed as "no output" only because
   `SroieReceipt.total` requires a positive number. They are now scored as `total` errors.
   2 have a negative gold total (DI matched it) and are excluded on both systems.

**Net effect on the headline.** The earlier "exact tie" (0.871 against 0.871) was partly the
date artifact. Self-hosted now leads overall, 0.914 against 0.868. The lead is carried by
`company` (+0.11) and `address` (+0.20). DI still leads on `total` (+0.10) and `date` (+0.02).

## Findings

- **Cost and coverage still separate the systems most.** $0.91 against $10.00 per 1k documents
  (11x). Self-hosted produced an answer for every receipt; DI returned no `Total` on 15 of 347
  (4.3%) and a wrong negative one on 4 more (1.2%).
- **`total` is the self-hosted weak spot.** 60 total misses out of 345: 48 read larger than
  gold (for example 200.0 against 169.8, 102.0 against 72.0) and 12 read smaller (6.0 against
  6.35). Reading the wrong amount on the receipt (cash tendered, a subtotal) fits the larger
  ones, but that was not checked receipt by receipt. It is a hypothesis, not a finding.
- **Latency is a wash** at single-request granularity (3893 ms against 3773 ms p50).
- **Data residency.** The case for self-hosting when documents cannot leave the company's
  infrastructure is unchanged and slightly stronger: no accuracy is given up overall, coverage
  is full, cost is an order of magnitude lower. A workload dominated by `total` extraction is
  where DI's typed output is a real advantage.

## Caveats

- One sample per receipt per system, default sampling temperature 0.7, no repeated trials.
  A 4.6-point overall gap is larger than the noise a single run usually carries, but this
  evidence alone does not give an error bar.
- The 4 wrong-negative DI rows have no stored DI company, address or date (the capture failed
  validation before saving them), so they count against DI's `total` only. Those three fields
  are simply absent for the 4 receipts.
- DI's F1 excludes the 15 receipts where it returned nothing, which slightly flatters DI's
  per-field numbers; coverage is stated beside them for that reason.
- The address word-overlap threshold (0.7) and the trailing-time pattern were both tuned by
  reading this same data, so they are not held-out choices.
- One config against one config: 4B reduced-res only. DI's cost is the published S0 rate, not
  what the run paid; self-hosted cost is this run's measured wall-clock at $0.000222/GPU-second.
